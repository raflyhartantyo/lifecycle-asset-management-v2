# Lifecycle Asset Management

Shared business language for LAM V2. These definitions distinguish current asset information, business activity and evidence supplied during verification.

## Organization and accountability

**Entity**:
A company organization that contains Functions and owns its scoped asset and inventory records.
_Avoid_: Building, tenant, Function

**Function**:
A business function belonging to exactly one Entity.
_Avoid_: Entity, location

**Business Owner**:
The accountable owner of particular assets, with relevant reporting and explicitly assigned approval responsibilities.
_Avoid_: Custodian, system administrator

**Custodian**:
The individual employee holding or using an asset; an employee awaiting directory registration can be recorded as pending.
_Avoid_: Business Owner, department, team

**Building**:
A shared physical location that may be used by several Entities and Functions, recorded without floor or room detail.
_Avoid_: Owning Entity, Function

## Register and transactions

**Asset**:
An individually identified company asset, including a documented internal software enhancement when classified as an asset.
_Avoid_: Inventory quantity, licence seat

**Asset Tag**:
The asset identifier generated by LAM, distinct from the code supplied by Accounting.
_Avoid_: Accounting Asset Code, serial number

**Accounting Asset Code**:
The company-wide unique accounting identifier available when an asset is registered.
_Avoid_: Asset Tag, Category abbreviation

**Category**:
One of Accounting's fixed classifications: Komputer (KO), Non-Komputer (NK) or Perangkat Lunak (PL).
_Avoid_: Asset Type, accounting account mapping

**Asset Type**:
A shared catalogue classification linked to one Category and usable by assets across Entities.
_Avoid_: Category, inventory item

**Asset Status**:
An asset's operational lifecycle state, distinct from Condition and the progress of a request.
_Avoid_: Condition, approval status

**Condition**:
The single asset classification with the choices Good, Broken and Ready to Auction.
_Avoid_: Asset Status, separate auction-readiness flag

**Inventory Item**:
A kind of stock managed by quantity rather than as individually tagged assets.
_Avoid_: Individual Asset

**Movement**:
A change to an asset's operational assignment or location; owner or Entity/Function changes require the applicable transfer approvals.
_Avoid_: Stock-verification correction, automatic transfer approval

**Direct Disposal**:
The authorized sale or non-sale retirement of one or more assets, retaining their identity and history.
_Avoid_: Disposal request, disposal approval

## Verification and reconciliation

**Stock Verification**:
A scoped exercise collecting assigned asset observations, reviewing outcomes and obtaining relevant Business Owner sign-off.
_Avoid_: Automatic master correction

**Respondent**:
A participant assigned to provide observations for particular assets in a stock-verification process.
_Avoid_: Permanent administrative role

**Verification Response**:
A respondent's assertion that assigned asset information is correct or their corrected observations of it.
_Avoid_: Verified master record, master-data update

**Verified Outcome**:
The recorded verification result under the agreed review criteria, distinct from merely receiving a response or reviewing it.
_Avoid_: Correct submitted, reviewed, acknowledged exception

**Reconciliation Finding**:
A recorded match, discrepancy or exception between an accounting-ledger input and register evidence for a comparison run.
_Avoid_: Register correction, approval

**Applied Correction**:
A reviewed, authorized change that has actually been written successfully to the relevant business record.
_Avoid_: Proposed correction, approved request, final sign-off

**Final Reconciliation Sign-off**:
Accounting/Governance's assigned decision on the actual reconciliation results after explicit correction application.
_Avoid_: Permission to bypass a transfer approval, proof of rollback

**Book Value**:
An asset's value calculated using the deferred accounting formula and adjusted by the scheduled monthly process.
_Avoid_: Automatically copied ledger amount, assumed purchase-cost balance
